### Important Notes for my personal usage
> [!NOTE]
> Contains notes which are useful to me, might also be useful for you if u have replicated my dotfiles on your setup. Also, these tips are to do the manual things after reinstall (thinking of automating it but ain't got time>_<).

- The easyeffects custom presets for earbuds and desk speakers are stored in ~/.config/easyeffects/.
- Make sure to follow the mkinitpio guide to switch to pywal theme in tty right after system boot.
- Remember to symlink scripts from ~/.scripts dir to $PATH by following the guide in that directory.
- Follow the guide to fix bootloader in the BIOS menu at ~/.efibootmgr directory.
- Remember to install the fonts from arch repos and AUR listed at ~/.fonts directory.
